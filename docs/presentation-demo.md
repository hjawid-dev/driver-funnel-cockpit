# Guided case demonstration

Open **Present** on the published app. This resets to fictional case data at Tuesday 09:30 and pauses the clock. The nine-step route takes about 6–8 minutes. Expand **Presenter guide: say, do, prove** for the script.

| Step | Demonstrate | Visible evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Julia's 09:30 incident | 25 minutes waiting, 12:00 deadline, pilot customer and 280 SEK margin |
| 2 | Run simulated calls and customer update | Driver retained, booking checked, site escalated, customer/account owner updated; clock jumps to 09:40 |
| 3 | Simulate keys found and incident closure | At 09:45, waiting compensation, cause and a timed 15-minute review; transport remains undelivered. Open the next new-site check and log the site call. |
| 4 | Explain the original funnel | 120 registrations, ~84 submissions, 25 active and 6-day mean; distinguished from the 44 illustrative records |
| 5 | Log Nora's reminder; open her phone | Exact missing document, preserved history and next driver action |
| 6 | Review Elin and record the onboarding decision | Written criteria, named owner, checks and first decision; original overdue registration deadline preserved |
| 7 | Book Elin's matching first job in the same call | A real reserved job, with Elin still accepted rather than active |
| 8 | Jump to pickup and depart; then deliver with feedback | Same driver and job; chronological pickup/departure/delivery; activation only after delivery |
| 9 | Open the weekly report | Flow versus cohorts, mean versus median, acceptance and quality guardrails; no claimed measured business uplift |

Navigation never performs a business action. Primary buttons explicitly simulate events or time jumps through the normal domain rules. Existing controls remain usable. Repeated actions do not duplicate events. Moving backwards preserves the session. If a later step needs earlier actions, **Prepare earlier demo actions** explicitly fills only missing prerequisites; it does not reset unrelated changes or invent delivery.

The no-keys-at-10:15 and borderline/no-job exceptions have optional explanations. The ordinary playbook and applicant controls remain available for interactive exploration. Late key discovery still requires a feasible revised plan. A manual rejection is preserved and cannot be replaced by the scripted acceptance.

Calls, SMS, identity/document checks, feedback and compensation are simulated with fictional data. Immediate keys at check-in are treated as a normal handover, not as an incident solved by the assistant. Leaving Present resumes time from the displayed moment. Use Present again for a clean rehearsal.
